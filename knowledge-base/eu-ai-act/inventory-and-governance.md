# Inventory and Governance

Within the Simpleact framework, inventory and governance are inseparable.

If a company cannot say which AI systems exist, who owns them, which provider they depend on, and what purpose they serve, it does not have an AI governance system.

Simpleact.de reinforces this logic at the platform level: central AI registration is the operating entry point for classification, compliance workflows, and exportable outputs.

## Inventory First

- AI tools and embedded AI features are listed
- owners, providers, and purposes are assigned
- deployment context is visible
- lifecycle status and review dates are maintained
- change triggers are documented

## Governance Second

- responsibilities are defined
- approvals are assigned
- review cycles are set
- inventory changes are tied to real business workflows such as procurement, release management, and vendor onboarding

## What Should Be Governed

The inventory should not be limited to formal "AI products". It should also cover:

- internal copilots and assistants
- embedded third-party AI functionality
- workflow automation using foundation models
- analytics or scoring systems with AI components
- AI APIs used inside broader software products

## Governance Outputs

A mature inventory supports:

- faster risk classification
- cleaner technical documentation
- clearer accountability
- stronger audit readiness
- better monitoring and reassessment discipline
