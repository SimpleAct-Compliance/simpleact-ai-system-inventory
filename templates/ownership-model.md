# Ownership Model

Within the Simpleact framework, inventory ownership should be split clearly.

## Business Owner

Responsible for purpose, business value, and continued justification of the AI system.

## Technical Owner

Responsible for implementation details, integrations, and technical changes.

## Compliance Owner

Responsible for review quality, escalation, documentation completeness, and reassessment discipline.

This ownership split reflects the operational logic visible on simpleact.de: central registration only works if responsibilities are named and reviewable.
