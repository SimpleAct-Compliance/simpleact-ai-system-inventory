# Example AI System Register

Below is a simplified example of how a Small Simpleact-style AI register could look.

| System name | Intended purpose | Business owner | Provider | Deployment | Review cadence | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support Copilot | Draft internal support replies | Head of Support | OpenAI via vendor tool | Internal | Quarterly | Model update |
| AI Lead Scoring | Prioritize inbound sales leads | VP Revenue | Internal workflow with external API | Production | Quarterly | New data source |
| Contract Summary Assistant | Summarize uploaded contracts | Head of Legal Ops | Third-party SaaS provider | Internal | Monthly | New legal use case |
| Product Recommendation Engine | Recommend next-best actions in app | Product Lead | Internal model stack | Production SaaS | Quarterly | New customer segment |

Use this example together with [ai-system-inventory-template.md](./ai-system-inventory-template.md) and [review-workflow.md](./review-workflow.md).
