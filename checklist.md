# Checklist

AI compliance is not a document, it is a system.

Within the Simpleact framework, this checklist mirrors the inventory-specific control layer of AI compliance. A good inventory should support the broader Simpleact system and the platform logic visible on simpleact.de: central visibility, structured assessment, and reviewable records.

## Inventory

- AI systems, tools, and providers are listed
- owners and purposes are defined
- deployment context is documented
- provider and model dependencies are recorded
- internal tools and external product features are both covered
- each inventory record has a review date

## Classification

- prohibited practice screening exists
- high-risk relevance is assessed
- assumptions and reasoning are recorded
- the inventory contains enough information to trigger classification work

## Governance

- roles and approvals are assigned
- review cadence exists
- human oversight is defined
- inventory updates are linked to onboarding, procurement, and product release workflows

## Documentation

- evidence-ready records exist
- templates are used consistently
- exports can support audits or internal review
- inventory data can feed technical documentation and audit exports

## Monitoring and Reporting

- incidents and changes are tracked
- reassessment triggers are defined
- reporting outputs can be generated
- material changes in provider, model, use case, or deployment trigger inventory updates
